1. Scope and Who We Are

Reviso is the publisher of the Reviso flashcard and study application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and its service providers collect, why it is used, where it is stored, when it is shared, and the choices available to you.

The App is intended only for people who are at least 18 years old. This policy applies to the App, its Railway-hosted backend, and the account, AI, advertising, analytics, and backup features described below.

2. Information We Collect and Store

2.1 Account and profile information

When you sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication, we receive your email address, display name, Google profile photo URL, sign-in provider information, and a Firebase user ID (UID). Firebase and Google also process authentication tokens and security metadata needed to sign you in. A profile image you choose inside the App is stored locally on your device.

You may provide a school or institution name, daily study goal, preferred study time, and onboarding answers such as subject, experience, study goal, study challenge, and how you heard about Reviso. Completion and update timestamps are also recorded.

2.2 Study data and data stored on your device

The App stores its main study data locally in an SQLite database. This includes:

  • Deck and chapter names, descriptions, icons, positions, identifiers, and timestamps
  • Flashcard questions and answers
  • Card state and spaced-repetition scheduling data, including due dates, ratings, review counts, stability, difficulty, steps, lapses, and last-review times
  • Review logs, daily study statistics, streak and goal progress, and records used to keep deletions consistent across backup and restore operations

The App also stores ordinary preferences and operational data on your device, such as theme, reminders and notification schedules, daily goal, school name, study time, onboarding state, profile-image path, cached credit information, pending reward-intent identifiers, backup timestamps, and temporary files. Typed answers are used during a review and are sent off the device only when you request AI scoring. Images are accessed only when you select or capture one for a feature that needs it.

2.3 Cloud Firestore and backend records

Reviso uses Cloud Firestore for the following UID-linked records:

  • Settings and onboarding: school name, daily goal, study time, onboarding answers and progress, completion state, and timestamps
  • Credits and access: credit balance, reset and reward timestamps, creation time, and any whitelist status
  • Reward verification and fraud prevention: reward-intent IDs, UID, status, expiry and processing times, AdMob transaction ID, ad unit, reward amount, and server-side verification callback timestamps; per-user cooldown state is also stored
  • AI reports: if you report an AI result, the UID, feature, selected report reason, generated output you reported, report status, report ID, and timestamps; per-user report cooldown state is also stored
  • Account deletion status: a minimal UID-keyed suppression and status record with request, update, and completion timestamps

The backend is hosted on Railway. It receives Firebase ID tokens and UIDs to authenticate requests, AI request content, credit and reward requests, report submissions, and account-deletion commands. Railway and the backend may process IP addresses, request paths, timestamps, response status, and other standard HTTP and security log data. Reviso does not intentionally store ordinary AI prompts, answers, or source images in its own backend database after a request, except that a generated AI output is stored when you deliberately submit that output as a report.

2.4 AI request data

For AI answer scoring, the App sends the flashcard question, reference answer, and the answer you typed to the Reviso backend. For image-to-flashcard generation, it sends the selected image, its media type, and instructions needed to generate cards. The backend sends this material to OpenRouter, which dynamically routes requests to downstream model providers. The model or provider handling a request can vary based on routing, availability, configuration, and fallback behavior.

Reviso does not use your content to train AI models. However, Reviso has not verified a binding zero-retention or no-training guarantee from OpenRouter or every dynamically selected downstream provider. OpenRouter and those providers may retain or use request data under their own terms, privacy policies, and routing settings. Do not submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive personal information to AI features.

2.5 Google Drive backups

After you authorize Google Drive access, the App can create backups manually and can run backups automatically. It keeps up to five versioned snapshots in the hidden Reviso app-data folder in your own Google Drive account. Each backup is a gzip-compressed JSON file containing decks, chapters, cards, daily statistics, review logs, sync-deletion records, and the backed-up daily-goal and school-name preferences.

The backup is not encrypted by the App. Google may apply its own transport and storage protections, but gzip is compression, not encryption. The Reviso backend cannot access your Drive backups; access is performed by the App using the Drive authorization you grant. Backups can remain in Google Drive after uninstalling the App unless they are separately deleted.

2.6 Analytics, device, and advertising data

Firebase Analytics may collect an app-instance identifier, the Firebase UID set after sign-in, app and device information, approximate location derived from network information, sessions, screens, and events. Events include sign-in and onboarding, deck and card creation, review activity and ratings, streaks and goals, AI feature use and scores, ad interactions, notification interactions, credits, and backup or restore activity. These events can include numeric identifiers and counts, but Reviso does not intentionally send flashcard text or typed answer text as analytics event parameters.

Google AdMob and its SDKs may process IP address, advertising or app identifiers, device and app information, diagnostic data, consent signals, and ad requests, impressions, clicks, video completion, and reward events. For rewarded ads, Google sends server-side verification data to Reviso so the backend can validate the reward and prevent duplicate or fraudulent credit claims.

  • Provide flashcards, reviews, spaced-repetition scheduling, reminders, settings, and cross-device account preferences
  • Process AI scoring and image-to-flashcard requests and return generated results
  • Operate credits, rewarded ads, server-side reward verification, abuse prevention, and deletion suppression
  • Create and restore Drive backups when authorized
  • Investigate voluntarily submitted AI reports and improve safety and quality
  • Measure reliability and feature usage, diagnose errors, and improve the App
  • Display, measure, and where permitted personalize advertising
  • Comply with law, enforce the Terms of Service, and protect users, Reviso, and service providers

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide the service you request, based on consent (for example, certain ad choices or Drive authorization), necessary for legitimate security and service-improvement interests, or required by law. You may withdraw consent where applicable, but a feature that needs the relevant data may then stop working.

5. AI Features and Reports

AI features are optional and consume credits. AI responses can be inaccurate, incomplete, unsafe, or unsuitable for your circumstances. Review generated cards and scores before relying on them.

AI request content passes through the Railway-hosted Reviso backend to OpenRouter and a dynamically selected downstream model provider. Reviso itself does not train models with your content. This statement does not create a no-retention or no-training promise on behalf of OpenRouter or downstream providers, and Reviso has no verified guarantee that every routed provider applies zero retention or excludes all training use.

If you choose Report AI output, the reported generated output, selected reason, feature, UID, status, and timestamps are stored so Reviso can review the report. Reporting is separate from ordinary AI processing and makes that output persist until account deletion.

6. Advertising and Privacy Choices

The App uses AdMob interstitial ads at transition points and optional rewarded ads that provide credits after successful verification. Declining a rewarded ad does not remove ordinary study access, although no reward is issued.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents or manages privacy and consent choices that govern how AdMob may serve ads. You can revisit available privacy choices in the App when that option is required and available. You can also use Android's advertising privacy and ad-personalization controls. Depending on your choice and region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized; ads may still appear after you decline personalization.

7. Storage, Security, and International Processing

Study content is primarily stored on your device. Account and operational records are stored with Firebase; the backend runs on Railway; AI content is processed through OpenRouter and downstream providers; analytics and ads are processed by Google; and authorized backups are stored in your Google Drive account.

Reviso uses HTTPS for network requests, Firebase ID-token authentication for backend endpoints, Firestore access controls, scoped Drive authorization, and server-side checks for credits and ad rewards. Drive backup files are gzip JSON and are not separately encrypted by the App. No security method is perfect, so Reviso cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and Google account protected.

These providers may process information in India, the United States, or other countries where they operate. Data-protection rules can differ between countries. Providers apply their own transfer mechanisms and safeguards as described in their policies.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local data. Study data, preferences, profile files, and caches remain on the device until you remove them, complete in-app account deletion, clear app data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling ordinarily clears Reviso's local app data only; it does not delete your Firebase account, Firestore/backend records, Analytics data, or Drive backups.

Firestore account records. There is no Firestore time-to-live (TTL) policy for Reviso's account records. Settings and onboarding data, credits, whitelist state, AI reports and report state, reward intents and reward state, and AdMob server-side verification or fraud-prevention records remain while the account is active. They are deleted as part of completed account deletion.

AI requests and hosting logs. Reviso does not intentionally retain ordinary AI request content in its own application database after returning the result, but OpenRouter, downstream model providers, Railway, and Google may retain request, diagnostic, or security data according to their own policies and legal obligations. Reviso cannot promise a specific upstream retention period or zero retention.

Drive backups. Up to five backup versions remain in the hidden Drive app-data folder until replaced, pruned, or deleted. In-app account deletion attempts to remove all Reviso Drive backups. If Drive authorization is unavailable, account deletion continues and the App provides instructions to remove the hidden app data manually from Google Account or Drive settings. The Reviso backend cannot perform that cleanup for you.

Analytics. On account deletion, Reviso submits deletion of linked Firebase Analytics user data through the Google Analytics Admin API and resets local analytics identifiers. Google may need time to process that request. Aggregate or de-identified measurements may remain under Google's retention practices and are not necessarily associated with your deleted account. In the rare case where an older App version already removed authentication before an interrupted Analytics request completed, Reviso displays a deletion reference and directs the user to the verified email support process to finish the request.

Deletion-integrity record. Reviso retains a minimal UID-keyed account-deletion suppression/status record, including status and request, update, and completion timestamps, indefinitely. This record prevents deleted data or delayed reward callbacks from recreating an account and supports resumable, verifiable deletion. It does not contain your flashcards, AI content, settings, credit balance, or email address.

Account deletion process. Deletion is network-dependent, occurs in stages, and is not instant. The process is designed to resume safely after interruption. It removes the Firebase Auth account, UID-linked settings and onboarding records, credits and whitelist state, AI reports and report state, reward intents and reward state, AdMob SSV transaction records, ordinary local app data, and accessible Drive backups, subject to the exceptions above. See the Account Deletion page for the complete process.

You may also request deletion by emailing studywithreviso@gmail.com. Reviso will process a valid email request within 30 days and may require identity verification before acting.
