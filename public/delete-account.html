Account Deletion

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Contact: studywithreviso@gmail.com

To request deletion of your Reviso account and all associated data:

  1. Email studywithreviso@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email address linked to your Reviso account in the body of the email.

Once we receive your request, we will delete the following within 30 days:

  • Your Firebase Auth record
  • Your Firestore credit data
  • Any other backend-stored data associated with your account

Locally stored data (flashcards, decks, review history, app preferences, and any cached content) is stored only on your device. This data is automatically removed when you uninstall the Reviso app. No further action is needed from you to remove local data.
