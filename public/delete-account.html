Delete in the App

The recommended method is the authenticated deletion flow:

  1. Open Reviso and select Profile.
  2. Select Delete Account and review the confirmation screens.
  3. Confirm deletion and complete Google re-authentication if requested.
  4. Keep a network connection while Reviso works through the deletion stages.

Deletion is network-dependent and is not instant. It runs in resumable stages so the App can continue after a timeout, connection failure, or restart. If deletion is interrupted, reopen Reviso and sign in with the same Google account if prompted to continue. Do not create new data while deletion is pending.

Data Deleted

When all applicable stages complete, Reviso deletes the following:

  • Firebase Authentication: your Reviso sign-in account and Firebase Auth UID account
  • Profile and settings: the Firestore users record, including school name, daily goal, study time, onboarding answers and progress, and related timestamps
  • Credits and access: user_credits and whitelisted_users records
  • AI reports: reported AI outputs, report reasons, feature, status, and timestamps in ai_reports, plus per-user report state in ai_report_users
  • Ad rewards and fraud-prevention data: ad_reward_intents, per-user state in ad_reward_users, and UID-linked admob_ssv_transactions, including transaction, reward, ad-unit, and callback-processing data
  • Local study database: decks, chapters, cards, daily statistics, review logs, scheduling data, and sync-deletion records
  • Other ordinary local app data: settings, onboarding state, cached credit and pending reward data, backup timestamps, profile images, migration copies, temporary files, and scheduled notifications

There is no Firestore TTL for the listed records while an account is active. The account-deletion process explicitly deletes them.

Google Drive Backups

If you authorized backups, Reviso may have stored up to five versioned gzip JSON snapshots in the hidden Reviso app-data folder in your Google Drive. The files are not encrypted by the App. The Reviso backend cannot access them.

In-app account deletion requires Google Drive access so all Reviso Drive backups can be removed. If Drive authorization is unavailable, expired, or denied, deletion stops and the App asks you to restore Drive access and try again. The Reviso backend cannot delete Drive files on your behalf.

To remove a backup manually:

  1. Open Google Account third-party connections and select Reviso.
  2. Remove Reviso's access and choose the option to delete its hidden app data if Google displays that option.
  3. If needed, open Google Drive settings, select Manage apps, find Reviso, and use Delete hidden app data.

Google may change the names or locations of these controls. Removing Drive authorization alone does not necessarily delete an existing backup.

Analytics Data

When your account is deleted, Reviso removes the analytics user identifier linked to your account and resets local analytics identifiers on your device. Firebase Analytics data already collected before deletion is not deleted by the App and remains subject to Google's retention practices, but Reviso no longer links new analytics activity to your deleted account.

Record Retained Indefinitely

Reviso retains one minimal Firestore account_deletions suppression/status record keyed by your Firebase UID. It contains deletion status and request, update, and completion timestamps. It is retained indefinitely to prevent delayed reward callbacks or other processes from recreating deleted data and to preserve resumable deletion integrity.

This retained marker does not contain your email address, settings, credit balance, flashcards, answers, images, AI outputs, or ad-reward transaction history.

Delete by Email

If you cannot use the App, email is an external alternative:

  1. Email studywithreviso@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request.
  2. Identify the Google email address used for your Reviso account.
  3. Complete any reasonable identity-verification step requested to protect the account from unauthorized deletion.

Reviso will process a valid email deletion request within 30 days. Do not send passwords, Google tokens, or identity documents unless Reviso specifically explains why a limited verification item is needed.

Uninstalling Is Not Account Deletion

Uninstalling Reviso ordinarily clears the App's local database, preferences, profile files, and caches from that device. It does not delete your Firebase Authentication account, Firestore/backend records, Firebase Analytics data, or Google Drive backups. Use the in-app deletion flow or the email alternative to delete the cloud account and associated data.
