Account Deletion

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Contact: studywithreviso@gmail.com

Delete from within the App (Recommended)

The easiest way to delete your account is directly from the App:

  1. Open Reviso and go to the Profile page.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm through the two-step dialog.
  4. You will be asked to sign in with Google once more to verify your identity before deletion proceeds.

Once confirmed, the following is deleted immediately and permanently:

  • Your Firebase Auth record (your account is removed)
  • Your Firestore data (user settings, credit records, and whitelist status)
  • All flashcards, decks, and review history stored locally on your device
  • All app preferences and cached data stored on your device
  • Your locally saved profile image

Firebase Analytics data linked to your account is submitted to Google for deletion. Google may take up to 180 days to fully remove this data from their systems.

Residual Server Data

Our backend server (hosted on Railway) stores credit records that are not yet automatically removed by the in-app deletion button. To ensure these records are also removed, please also email us using the instructions below.

Delete by Email (Alternative or for Server Data Removal)

If you are unable to access the app, or if you want to ensure all server-side credit records are removed:

  1. Email studywithreviso@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email address linked to your Reviso account.

We will manually process your request within 30 days and confirm once complete.

Locally Stored Data

Flashcards, decks, review history, and app preferences are stored only on your device. This data is automatically removed when you delete your account using the in-app button, or when you uninstall the App. No further action is needed to remove local data.
